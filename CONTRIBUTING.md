If you’re looking at this file, it means you want to contribute to the project.

First of all, thank you very much! Secondly, any contribution, no matter how small, is welcome.

There is one simple rule to follow: no vibe-coding and no AI in any part of the project.

The project’s philosophy is zero AI. I don’t want to hear excuses like “I don’t have time.” I have time to dedicate to the project, I don’t have a job, I don’t have a girlfriend, and I live with my mom. So the excuse “AI helps me save time” doesn’t apply here. Please respect this philosophy.

However, AI is allowed for fixing typos and for language translation.
